03About

Give me the messy problem.
I'll bring back a system that works.

Having lived across different cities and cultures, I'm comfortable with new environments and new perspectives. Now I'm in Punjab, pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science, specialising in Data Science and AI, at Lovely Professional University.

Beyond academics, I'm usually deep in side projects, podcasts or emerging tech. I'm drawn to what's next, and I like digging beneath the surface to see what makes things tick.

Lately, most of my work sits in applied AI: LLM-powered apps, RAG and multi-agent systems. The highlight so far: winning Best Pipeline at the TransOrg AgentIQ Datathon with my team, for a five-agent LLM risk desk on a graph-analytics fraud pipeline that uncovered 121 dispute rings in 20K UPI payments. I've also trained LightGBM models on 600K+ rows of quick-commerce data, built a TypeScript Monte Carlo engine, and written Dijkstra, A*, Edmonds–Karp and Tarjan's algorithm from scratch in C++17.

I tend to ask “why” one more time than people expect, and I'll try the other way first, just to be sure. Half the time it's a dead end; the other half is where the good ideas come from.
